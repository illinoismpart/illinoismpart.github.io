About MPART

Learn about our mission, focus areas, and how we work.

Our Mission

MPART is a student-powered Medicaid policy lab that produces standardized, high-quality applied Medicaid policy research that supports real-world decision-making while developing future policy leaders.

MPART conducts applied research at the intersection of health policy and emerging technology, producing credible, actionable intelligence for policymakers, state agencies, and their partners.

What We Focus On

Applied Research

Usable outputs that inform real policy decisions. We don't produce research for the shelf — we build tools and analysis that agencies can act on.

Data Integrity

Consistent, standardized data across all projects. Every dataset follows the same validation and documentation standards so findings are comparable and reproducible.

Workforce Development

Preparing students for careers in health policy and public service. Our researchers gain hands-on experience with real policy problems and professional tools.

Collaborative Publishing

Partnerships with policy fellows and external stakeholders. We co-produce outputs with agencies and academic partners across the University of Illinois System.

How We Work

MPART operates through a standardized research production model designed to ensure consistency, accuracy, and repeatability across all outputs. Work is structured into distinct phases:

Research Phases

Topic — Define the policy question and scope

Design — Develop methodology and data collection plan

Collect — Gather and process data from public sources

Validate — Structured review, data validation, and quality checks

Curate — Final review by senior research staff prior to publication

All outputs undergo structured review including data validation and final review by senior research staff prior to publication.

Institutional Home

MPART is a unit of the University of Illinois Springfield. We are located in downtown Springfield at the Horace Mann Building.
