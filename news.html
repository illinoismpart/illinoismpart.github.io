August 2026

Illinois Trail at the Illinois State Fair

MPART built Illinois Trail, a browser game for the Illinois Department of Healthcare and Family Services that reworks the classic Oregon Trail as a drive down Route 66 from Chicago to East St. Louis. Each leg of the trip stops on a situation a family might actually face, such as medical debt after a hospital stay, and the choice a player makes opens into an explanation of the Medicaid and child support programs HFS administers and of the state's own history in healthcare. The route is deliberate: 2026 is Route 66's hundredth year.

The game ran at the HFS booth in the Governor's Tent at the 2026 Illinois State Fair, in Springfield from August 13 to 23 and in DuQuoin from August 28 to September 7. It was built by Oladimeji M. Adaramewa, an MPART graduate researcher.

April 2026

Quantized MedGemma 27B Released for Local Inference

MPART published a 4-bit GPTQ quantization of Google's MedGemma 27B instruction-tuned text model. Quantizing to 4 bits cuts the memory the weights occupy to roughly a quarter of the full-precision version, which brings a 27-billion-parameter medical model within reach of a single GPU.

The aim is practical: work that touches sensitive material can run on hardware a research team controls rather than through a commercial API. The model is released on Hugging Face under the terms of Google's Health AI Developer Foundations license.

September 2025

MPART Launches at UIS

The Medicaid Policy Applied Research Team launched in September 2025 as a new applied research initiative within the University of Illinois Springfield. MPART brings together faculty researchers, graduate students, and applied technology to produce credible policy intelligence for state agency partners.
