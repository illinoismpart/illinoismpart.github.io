September 2025

MPART Launches at UIS

The Medicaid Policy Applied Research Team launched in September 2025 as a new applied research initiative within the University of Illinois Springfield. MPART brings together faculty researchers, graduate students, and applied technology to produce credible policy intelligence for state agency partners.
